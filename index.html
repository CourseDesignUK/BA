INTEGRATED ELECTRONIC & PNEUMATIC ARCHITECTURE

The Dräger PSS® 7000 integrates power supply, pressure reducer, and telemetry hoses directly into the carbon-fiber backplate cavity to eliminate snagging hazards in structural firefighting environments.

BACKPLATE WITH MONITOR: 4.40 kg
1ST STAGE OUTPUT PRESSURE: 7.5 bar (Nominal)

HOSE PROTECTION SLEEVES: Reflective & Photoluminescent

Instructor Note // Modularity

All electronic modules and pneumatic hoses feature a central backplate power supply module and snap-fit routing channels so gauges and lung demand valves can be configured for left- or right-shoulder preference.

STAGE 02 // ERGONOMIC CARRIER ASSET: body.glb

3-POINT HEIGHT-ADJUSTABLE CARBON BACKPLATE

To ensure optimal load distribution on the iliac crest (hips) rather than the firefighter’s spine, the high-strength carbon-fiber composite backplate pairs a 3-position torso height adjustment with a dynamically sliding and pivoting waist belt.

01 // VULCANISED ARAMID HARNESS WEBBING

Compression-molded cushioning with high-temperature Aramid blend webbing and high-friction anti-slip shoulder surfaces resist wear, chemical ingress, and heat.

02 // SLIDE-AND-LOCK TOOL-FREE RELEASE

Shoulder and waist harness attachments disconnect from the backplate without tools via Dräger's slide-and-lock mechanism for rapid post-incident decontamination.

03 // SNAP-FIT 1ST STAGE PRESSURE REDUCER

Secured by a single retaining pin, allowing rapid replacement of the complete pneumatic assembly in the workshop.

STAGE 03 // HIGH-PRESSURE VESSEL ASSET: tank.glb

6.8L 300 BAR CARBON COMPOSITE & QUICKCONNECT

Constructed with a seamless aluminum liner overwrapped in carbon and fiberglass filaments within an epoxy matrix, the 6.8L / 300 bar cylinder reduces weight by up to 50% compared to steel vessels while storing 2,040 litres of breathable air.

WORKING / TEST PRESSURE: 300 bar / 450 bar
NOMINAL AIR CAPACITY: 2,040 Litres (6.8L × 300 bar)
QUICKCONNECT INTERLOCK: Locks at >5 bar (~70 psi)
CYLINDER STRAP SYSTEM: Single or Twin Cylinder Cam-Lock
SAFETY PROTOCOL // QUICKCONNECT COUPLING
Quarter-turn QuickConnect allows cylinder swaps in seconds via a push-and-turn action. A pneumatic safety lock prevents disconnection while the reducer is pressurized above 5 bar. Always bleed system via the LDV before uncoupling.
STAGE 04 // RESPIRATORY INTERFACE ASSET: mask.glb

DRÄGER FPS® 7000 FULL-FACE MASK, LDV & HUD

The Dräger FPS® 7000 full-face mask provides a distortion-free polycarbonate visor with a 90% field of vision, a hypoallergenic EPDM double-sealing facial skirt (Sizes S, M, L), and an ergonomic 5-point head harness or helmet-mask adapter.

PLUS LUNG DEMAND VALVE (LDV): Balanced piston design delivers >1,000 L/min on first inhalation with positive-pressure standby switch-off and front bypass purge button.
WIRELESS FPS® 7000 HEAD-UP DISPLAY: Snaps inside the mask without tools. Automatically pairs via short-range encrypted radio with the Bodyguard® 7000, auto-adjusting LED brightness to ambient smoke/fire conditions.
