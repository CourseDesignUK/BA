Section 02 — Carrying System & Harness

Carbon-Composite Backplate & Dynamic Harness

The carrying frame is engineered from an anti-static carbon-fiber composite with exceptional heat, impact, and chemical resistance—transferring equipment weight directly onto the firefighter’s hips rather than the shoulders.

3-Position Height Adjustment

Twin sliding stainless-steel latches allow rapid adjustment between Short, Medium, and Long torso settings to match every wearer’s stature.

Pivoting & Sliding Waist Pad

A flexible stainless-steel pivot mechanism allows the waist belt to rotate and slide dynamically as the firefighter bends, climbs ladders, or crawls through tight openings.

Vulcanized Chloroprene Rubber Harness

Fitted with high-visibility reflective strips and spring-loaded quick-release shoulder and waist buckles for tool-free removal, rapid servicing, and thorough decontamination.

Integrated Hose Routing Channels

Stainless-steel channels recessed inside the backplate protect medium-pressure air and telemetry cables from snagging in confined spaces.

Section 03 — Compressed Air & Pneumatics

300 bar Carbon Cylinder & Pressure Reducer

Breathable air is stored in a lightweight seamless aluminum-lined vessel overwrapped with carbon and fiberglass filaments, paired with a fail-safe single-stage balanced piston pressure reducer.

Single-Stage Balanced Piston Reducer

Steps 300 bar cylinder pressure down to a stable 6.5 – 8.0 bar supply regardless of remaining cylinder pressure. Includes an over-pressurization relief valve (>11 bar) and dual-side medium-pressure ports for decontamination or rescue airline attachment.

55 ± 5 bar Pneumatic Warning Whistle

Activates automatically when cylinder pressure drops to 55 ± 5 bar, generating an unmistakable acoustic warning of >90 dBA while consuming less than 5 L/min of air.

Section 04 — Mask, Lung Demand Valve & HUD

Dräger FPS® 7000 Mask & Wireless HUD

The FPS® 7000 full-face mask maintains positive pressure inside the facepiece so smoke and toxic gases cannot enter even during peak physical exertion.

Plus-Pressure Lung Demand Valve (LDV)

Available with push-in A connection (EN 148-1) or ESA coupling. Activates positive pressure automatically upon the first inhalation, delivers >500 L/min peak airflow, and features a front reset button to shut off positive pressure when doffing plus a red bypass override button.

FPS® 7000 Facepiece Architecture

Features a double-seal EPDM or Silicone face blank, a distortion-free Class 1 optical polycarbonate visor, an integrated stainless-steel speech diaphragm for clear voice communication, and a 5-point Kevlar/aramid or rubber head harness.

Wireless Head-Up Display (HUD)

Pairs wirelessly with the Bodyguard® 7000 and automatically adapts LED brightness to ambient light:

● ● ● ● 4 Green LEDs 75% – 100% (225 – 300 bar)
● ● ● ○ 3 Green LEDs 50% – 75% (150 – 225 bar)
● ● ○ ○ 2 Yellow LEDs 25% – 50% (75 – 150 bar)
● ○ ○ ○ 1 Red LED < 25% (~75 bar)
★ ○ ○ ○ Flashing Red + Whistle < 55 bar (Retreat Immediately)
